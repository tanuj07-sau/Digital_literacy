# Stay Safe Online – Prevention Checklist for College Students in India

## General Online Safety

| # | Prevention Tip |
|---|----------------|
| 1 | **Never click links in unsolicited emails or SMS.** Always go directly to the official website by typing the URL in your browser. |
| 2 | **Check the sender's email address carefully.** Phishing emails often use addresses like `support@sbi-help.com` instead of `@sbi.co.in`. |
| 3 | **Look for HTTPS** in the website URL and a padlock icon before entering any personal information. |
| 4 | **Use strong, unique passwords** for every account. A strong password has at least 8 characters with uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols. |
| 5 | **Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)** on all important accounts — Gmail, Instagram, GitHub, LinkedIn, and banking apps. |
| 6 | **Never share your OTP with anyone** — not even someone claiming to be from your bank, telecom company, or the government. |
| 7 | **Keep your phone and apps updated.** Software updates often contain security patches that protect you from known vulnerabilities. |
| 8 | **Avoid using public Wi-Fi for sensitive tasks.** If you must, use a VPN (Virtual Private Network) to encrypt your connection. |

## UPI and Financial Safety (Specific to Indian Students)

| # | UPI / Payment Safety Tip |
|---|--------------------------|
| 9 | **Never scan a QR code sent by a stranger** to "receive" money — scanning a QR code can only send money from your account, never receive it. |
| 10 | **Do not share your UPI PIN or UPI ID** with anyone. Your UPI PIN is only needed when you are sending money — not receiving it. |

## Reporting Cybercrime in India

If you are a victim of any cybercrime — phishing, UPI fraud, identity theft, or cyberbullying:

- 🌐 **Online Portal:** [cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in) — file a complaint 24/7
- 📞 **Helpline Number:** **1930** — available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week
- 🏛️ **Local Police:** File an FIR at your nearest police station under the IT Act, 2000
