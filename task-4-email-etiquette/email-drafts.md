## Email 1: Requesting an Assignment Extension

**Subject:** Request for Extension – [Assignment Name] – [Your Name] – [Reg No.]

Dear Professor [Last Name],

I hope this email finds you well. I am writing to respectfully request a short extension for the submission of [Assignment/Project Name], which is currently due on [original due date].

I have been working consistently on this assignment; however, I have encountered [briefly state reason: e.g., a technical issue with the required software / a health concern / unexpected overlap with another deadline]. I believe an additional [2–3] days would allow me to submit work that properly reflects my understanding of the course material.

I understand that extensions are not always possible and I completely respect your decision either way. If an extension is not feasible, I will submit what I have by the original deadline.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Warm regards,
[Your Full Name]
[Registration Number]
[Branch and Year]
[Course Code and Name]
[College Email Address]

## Report Write-Up (100–150 words — paste this in your project report)

For Task 4, I drafted two professional emails following standard email etiquette guidelines. The first email, addressed to a professor, requests an extension politely while acknowledging the professor's authority and offering an alternative. The second email is addressed to an internship coordinator and follows the structure of a formal job inquiry: a clear introduction, relevant skills, and a respectful closing.

One real-world scenario where poor digital communication caused a problem is the case of employees who have been dismissed after sending unprofessional messages on company email, forgetting that all workplace emails are logged and monitored. In one widely reported situation, a new employee was let go after sending a casual, sarcastic email to a senior manager instead of a colleague. What could have been done differently is simple: read the email once before sending, check the recipient, and maintain professional language at all times, regardless of the platform.
