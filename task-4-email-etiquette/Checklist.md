# Social Media Do's and Don'ts for College Students

## DO's 

| # | Do |
|---|-----|
| 1 | **Use a professional profile photo** on LinkedIn - a clear, well-lit headshot with a plain background. |
| 2 | **Think before you post** - ask yourself if you would be comfortable with your future employer or professor seeing this. |
| 3 | **Keep your privacy settings updated** - review who can see your posts, photos, and personal information regularly. |
| 4 | **Use your real name on professional platforms** (GitHub, LinkedIn, Kaggle) so recruiters can find you easily. |
| 5 | **Credit others' work** when sharing articles, images, or ideas - always mention the original creator. |
| 6 | **Report cyberbullying or harassment** using the platform's reporting tools and save evidence (screenshots) before reporting. |
| 7 | **Separate personal and professional accounts** - what you share with friends need not be visible to your academic network. |

## DON'Ts 

| # |Don't |
|---|-------|
| 1 | **Don't share personal details publicly** - your phone number, home address, Aadhaar number, or bank details should never appear on social media. |
| 2 | **Don't post during exams or class hours** - it reflects poorly on your discipline and academic seriousness. |
| 3 | **Don't engage in online arguments or hate speech** - even if you delete a post, others may have already screenshotted it. |
| 4 | **Don't click on suspicious links** shared via DMs, even from friends - their account may have been hacked. |
| 5 | **Don't use the same password** across multiple platforms - if one account is hacked, all others become vulnerable. |
| 6 | **Don't overshare your location** in real time - posting "at the airport" or "alone at home" can create safety risks. |
| 7 | **Don't post or share unverified news** - always fact-check information before sharing, especially during sensitive events. |
