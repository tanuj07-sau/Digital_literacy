## Module Summaries

### Task 1 – Digital Literacy Awareness Infographic
Created a one-page infographic using **Canva** covering what digital literacy means, safe internet practices, and professional online presence. Exported and added to repository.

### Task 2 – Student Digital Portfolio
Set up professional profiles on **GitHub**, **LinkedIn**, and **Kaggle**. Each platform builds a distinct part of my academic and professional digital identity.

### Task 3 – Coding & Collaboration Platforms
Completed a beginner challenge on **HackerRank** and created a 5-question **Google Form** Digital Literacy Quiz for batchmates.

### Task 4 – Professional Email & Etiquette Guide

Case study on **Phishing** with a 10-point "Stay Safe Online" prevention checklist for Indian college students including UPI safety and reporting channels.
